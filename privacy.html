Privacy

Plain-language information about what this website currently collects and what will change when direct enquiry delivery is connected.

Current rolling preview

The current enquiry form prepares a summary in your browser. It does not transmit the form contents to Akari Studio or a third-party form provider.

Hosting

This preview is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information required to deliver the website. Refer to GitHub’s own privacy documentation for details.

Fonts

The preview currently requests the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. The production plan is to self-host the required font files before the final custom-domain launch.

Analytics and cookies

No Akari analytics, advertising cookies or behavioural profiling have been added to this preview.

Enquiry delivery before launch

Before direct form delivery is enabled, this notice will be updated with the data controller’s contact route, the form provider, what information is retained, why it is used and how long it is kept.

Your choices

Do not include confidential or sensitive personal information in a website-project enquiry. Once direct delivery is connected, you will be able to ask for access, correction or deletion through the published Akari contact route.
