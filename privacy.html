Privacy

Plain-language information about what this website does—and does not do—with the details you enter.

Contacting Akari

The project-preparation form works locally in your browser. When you choose Continue in your email app, it prepares an email addressed to hello@akaristudio.co.uk using the details you entered.

The website does not send those details, store them in a database or confirm delivery. Nothing reaches Akari until you review and send the email from your own email application.

Information you choose to email

The prepared email may contain your name, reply email, business name, current website, preferred timing, broad website route and the opportunity you want to discuss. Do not include confidential or sensitive personal information.

Email handling

If you send the prepared email—or contact Akari directly—your email provider and Akari’s Google Workspace service will process the message so it can be delivered and answered. Your own email application controls whether a draft opens and whether you send it.

Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information required to deliver the site. Refer to GitHub’s privacy information for its own handling.

Fonts

The website requests the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. That request may share technical connection information with Google as part of delivering the font.

Analytics and cookies

Akari has not added analytics, advertising cookies, behavioural profiling or tracking pixels to this website.

Your choices

You can avoid the preparation form and email hello@akaristudio.co.uk directly. If you have already contacted Akari and want to ask about information in that correspondence, use the same address.
