A useful fit check

Best for businesses with something real to reveal.

Akari is a strong fit if…

  • Your work or reputation is stronger than your current website.
  • You want better-fit enquiries, not just more traffic.
  • You value originality but need customers to feel comfortable.
  • You can support claims with real work, reviews or expertise.

Probably not the right fit if…

  • The only priority is the lowest possible price.
  • You want invented proof or misleading claims.
  • A complex web application is the primary requirement.
  • You need a large agency team or full marketing department.
